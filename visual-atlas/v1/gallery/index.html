The city is incomplete.
The evidence is real.

These images use archived Hong Kong survey maps, a reconstructed surface from two November 1993 aerial photographs, and a separately dated modern building inventory. No missing historical buildings have been invented.

Blank spaces and broken surfaces mean missing observations, not an empty historical city.

The warm light, exhibition supports and virtual viewpoints are design choices. They do not recreate the weather or cockpit view of a particular flight.

How to read this atlas

History, reconstruction, illustration.

A · Observations

Dated map marks and photographs are source observations. They still inherit source accuracy, registration and date limitations.

B · Reconstruction

The 1993 surface is an estimate under nominal camera calibration. Current building masses use approximate elevations. Historical outline heights remain unknown.

C · Missing evidence

Unobserved roofs, walls and city blocks are not filled. Neutral exhibition supports are not measured terrain or sea level.

The surface preserves 225,371 original triangles. Its elevations are relative to a fitted local park/road plane, not Hong Kong Principal Datum. The 2026 building panels have their own HKPD elevations. Map editions do not establish exact construction or demolition dates.

The A36143 date-reading history is preserved in the source register: an earlier ambiguous reading was corrected to 2 November 1993 after inspection of the original image and catalogue.
