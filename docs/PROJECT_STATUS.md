## Established within declared domains

- Complete constructed calm-air and steady-wind landing scenarios, each with its
  own continuous-time model-feasibility certificate. These are distinct scenarios,
  not an identified causal comparison of weather.
- Restricted analytical identities, explicit approximations and a finite-family
  calm normalized-margin bound. Earlier invalid global-exclusion claims remain withdrawn.
- A separately published [assumption-conditional local certificate](../research/checkers-runway13/integrated/assumption_solution/refinement/local_certificate/REPORT.md):
  strict local primary optimum in five flare variables and non-strict local primary
  optimum in the twelve-parameter family. Ten condition points have certified paths;
  global primary/secondary optimality and a complete condition map remain unresolved.
- Acquired historical map/photo records, conditional stereo geometry, dated
  obstacle observations and source-linked visualizations.
- Hash-verified versioned distribution of permitted evidence and editable atlas scenes.

## Open scientific dependencies

Historical clearance remains **blocked**. Joint runway/procedure registration,
complete dated obstacle volumes and height bounds, absolute datum calibration,
aircraft/contact/control/tracking calibration and uncertainty propagation remain
incomplete. Modern geography does not supply missing 1993 truth. A recorded
runway-end discrepancy is not permission to add landing distance.

The [integration traceability](../research/checkers-runway13/integrated/TRACEABILITY.md)
and [records follow-up](../research/checkers-runway13/integrated/records_followup/REPORT.md)
identify the evidence gates. The historical closed-form optimum, full robust-gust
policy and go-around viability are not solved by the current certificates.
The conditional 5 kt tailwind and 60/65 m urban cases remain unresolved, not proved
infeasible. The new local theorem does not close these historical evidence gates.

Archive provenance retains 2,739 warnings and source-specific restrictions.
Three inherited v1 paper manifest discrepancies remain documented; frozen hashes
are not regenerated to conceal them. The atlas's original browser-interaction gap was subsequently checked in local
Chromium on desktop/mobile; no deployed-site acceptance is claimed. See [migration validation](migration/VALIDATION.md)
for newly executed checks rather than interpreting old receipts as live results.
