# Source and rights guide

[Project guide](README.md) · [Archive rights](../archive/licensing/README.md)

This page routes to existing rights authorities. It grants no new permissions.

| Material | Authority and boundary |
|---|---|
| Original research/code | [NOTICE](../NOTICE.md); no blanket open-source licence selected |
| Permitted archive data | [Rights registry](../archive/licensing/rights_registry.json), [release manifest](../archive/releases/v1.0.0/manifest.json) and collection attribution |
| DAP-L0 aerials and Old Survey Sheets | Specific audited open-data acquisition routes; not a blanket permission for paid Lands imagery or all HKMS previews |
| Weather records | NOAA/NCEI source identity and format documentation retained in the evidence/archive records |
| CAD/AIS/Boeing, scholarly scans and unresolved sources | Metadata/citations or derived facts only where recorded; independently obtain restricted originals for dependent calculations |
| Paper figures | [Version-specific rights and provenance](../research/checkers-runway13/whitepaper/v4/RIGHTS_AND_PROVENANCE.md) |
| Atlas images/scenes | [Atlas source audit](../visual-atlas/v1/metadata/SOURCE_AUDIT.md) and [rights records](../visual-atlas/v1/metadata/rights.json) |

The wider catalogue and the permitted public distribution are different sets.
An acquisition URL, citation, historical upload or accessible webpage does not
establish a new redistribution licence. Proposed original-work licences under
`archive/licensing/` remain inactive until explicitly granted by the author.

## Historical source documentation

[DATA_SOURCES.md](../DATA_SOURCES.md) describes the dated research snapshot and
its exclusions. Its statement that v3 was unfinished belongs to that earlier
integration checkpoint; it does not override the [current publication index](../papers/README.md).
Likewise old “publication pending” statements in archive preparation records do
not undo the subsequently published archive or activate proposed licences.

Private enquiries, correspondence, credentials, dynamic download sessions and
locally retained third-party scans remain excluded. Do not copy workstation
folders or private Git-history backups into a new release. Use the existing
source-specific rights records and exact inclusion manifests.

The [archive readiness report](../archive/PUBLICATION_READINESS.md) retains
unresolved provenance warnings. Their continued visibility is part of the
research record, not a reason to fabricate missing lineage.
