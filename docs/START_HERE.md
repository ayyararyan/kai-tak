# Five minutes at Kai Tak

[Home](../README.md) · [Project guide](README.md)

## Discover

**First minute:** open [Checkers with Runway 13](../papers/README.md). Its abstract
and opening pages explain the historical approach and the complete-landing question.

**Next two minutes:** explore the [visual atlas](../visual-atlas/README.md).
Start with *November 1993, in fragments*, then *From photograph to spatial evidence*.
The holes and missing heights are part of the evidence, not unfinished artwork.

## Investigate

The [archive catalogue](../archive/CATALOGUE.md) organizes historical geography,
aviation evidence and mathematical research into nine collections. A catalogue
record may describe a restricted or unavailable source; it is not always a download.
Follow the [research map](RESEARCH_MAP.md) to see which observations feed which results.

## Reproduce

Use the [reproduction guide](REPRODUCIBILITY.md) to choose a task: check metadata,
test a model, rebuild a manuscript, restore data, or recover an editable scene.
These tasks have different inputs and costs. You do not need Blender or a large
data download to read the paper or run ordinary repository checks.

Before treating a result as historical truth, read the short
[current-status page](PROJECT_STATUS.md). For a new investigation, begin with the
[laboratory index](../research/README.md) and [contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.md).
