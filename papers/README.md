## What changed

- **v5:** a full narrative rewrite and fifteen redesigned scientific illustrations,
  preserving the underlying models and evidence. The published edition has 38 pages;
  its first-person foreword remains marked for author review. [Publication record](../research/checkers-runway13/whitepaper/v5/PUBLICATION.md).
- **v4:** RFS author-format adaptation of v3; unchanged scientific content, sixteen
  figures, seven worked insights and six mathematical appendices. This is not an
  RFS publication or endorsement. The foreword remains marked for author review.
- **v3:** illustrated narrative and mathematical exposition, integrating the frozen
  evidence available to that manuscript. [Version record](../research/checkers-runway13/whitepaper/v3/VERSION_CHANGES.md).
- **v2:** complete declared steady-wind landing and its own feasibility certificate,
  alongside the separate calm scenario.
- **v1:** retained historical draft. It is not promoted to accepted publication status.
