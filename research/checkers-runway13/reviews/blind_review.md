- **P2 — Ground-obstacle diagnostics do not participate in the advertised mesh refinement.** `model/trajectory.py:180–189,208,229–233`; `model/solve.py:100,120–121`. Every evaluation uses `rollout(q,p)` with its default 101 samples, irrespective of the flight-grid `n`. Thus the coarse/dense mesh comparison can agree while a cylinder intersecting the straight ground path is completely missed, and increasing `--samples` cannot improve that check. Snapshot-only execution with `BASE_Q` and a cylinder at `(1261.5782732546845,0)`, radius 1 m, height 2 m, clearance 0 returned a positive obstacle margin `0.0412163528871298` at all of `n=121,1201,12001`, although the rollout necessarily passes through its center; its nearest rollout sample is 17.4865 m away. The finite-grid disclaimer avoids a continuous-certificate claim, but the convergence diagnostic does not expose or refine this ground-phase blind spot. Compute analytic cylinder intersection for the straight monotone rollout, or independently refine ground sampling and report its resolution/convergence. Add this between-samples regression case.

- **P2 — “plus5/minus5” wind sensitivity cases overwrite the nominal wind instead of perturbing it.** `model/solve.py:106–107`. The accepted custom-config path allows arbitrary constant 3-vector winds, but these cases always install `[0,5,0]` and `[0,-5,0]`. With nominal `[8,3,1]`, their actual changes are `[-8,2,-1]` and `[-8,-8,-1]`: both silently remove headwind and vertical wind. With nominal `[0,5,0]`, the plus5 case is unchanged and minus5 is a −10 m/s perturbation. Therefore these case names cannot consistently describe local ±5 m/s crosswind sensitivities. Preserve the nominal x/z components and add ±5 to nominal y; alternatively explicitly name them absolute replacement-wind scenarios and record the nominal vector and delta. Verify using a nonzero nominal wind.
