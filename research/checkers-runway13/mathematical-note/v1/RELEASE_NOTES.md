A 36-page companion to *Checkers with Runway 13*, V5, in its original academic format. The note develops the analytical bounds and computer-assisted proofs for the declared A1 constrained hybrid landing model.

Its four principal theorems establish a unique root in an exact decimal box, strict local primary optimality in five flare variables, continuous-time feasibility of the connected landing, and non-strict local primary optimality in the twelve-parameter polynomial family. The certified local value is approximately 0.1523607643018617; the global-family bracket is [0.15236076430186170190, 0.181970215]. The upper bound is not attained by the certified construction.

The package includes editable LaTeX, six reproducible mathematical figures, a pinned public proof-source subset, exact matrix data, verification scripts and logs, independent review reports, and a theorem/evidence ledger. Fresh verification passed 117 public tests, three wind-domain certificates and ten condition certificates. Seven tests needing restricted or original-workspace inputs are explicitly excluded.

Secondary primary-value retention is certified within 0.0001; secondary-effort optimality is not established. The result is conditional on the mathematical model and is neither a globally optimal landing solution nor a historical/operational aircraft certificate.
