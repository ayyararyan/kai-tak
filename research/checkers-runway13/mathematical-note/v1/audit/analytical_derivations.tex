\subsection*{Exact polynomial regularity calculation}
Write the scalar Hermite basis as
\[
 H(s)=10s^3-15s^4+6s^5,\quad
 A(s)=s-6s^3+8s^4-3s^5,\quad
 B(s)=-4s^3+7s^4-3s^5.
\]
Let $b=64s^3(1-s)^3$ and $o=(2s-1)b$.  For a polynomial
$p(s)=\sum_{k=0}^n c_ks^k$ and rational cell $[a,a+h]$, substitution
$s=a+ht$ gives power coefficients
\[
 d_j=h^j\sum_{k=j}^n c_k\binom{k}{j}a^{k-j}.
\]
The corresponding degree-$n$ Bernstein coefficients are
\[
 \beta_i=\sum_{j=0}^i d_j\frac{\binom{i}{j}}{\binom{n}{j}},
 \qquad 0\le i\le n.
\]
Indeed $t^j=\sum_{i=j}^n\binom{i}{j}\binom{n}{j}^{-1}
\binom ni t^i(1-t)^{n-i}$.  Since the Bernstein basis is nonnegative
and sums to one on $[0,1]$, $p([a,a+h])$ lies between the smallest
and largest $\beta_i$.  This is an exact rational enclosure when
the input data are rational; no sampled polynomial values are used.

Apply the formula to $H',A',B',b',o'$ on the 128 cells
$[i/128,(i+1)/128]$.  The maximum absolute enclosure endpoints are
respectively
\[
 \frac{15}{8},\quad1,\quad1,\quad
 \frac{288141339}{83886080},\quad2.
\]
The fixed entry/gate vertical endpoint speeds obey
$|v_z|<5$, verified by outward evaluation of
$153k\exp(z/17000)\sin(3.1^\circ)$ at $z=146,21.24$.
The approach parameter bounds therefore imply
\begin{align*}
 |\dot z_a|
 &\le\frac{124.76}{30}\frac{15}{8}
 +5(1+1)+\frac{50}{30}
 \left(\frac{288141339}{83886080}+2\right)\\
 &=\frac{33792275107}{1258291200}.
\end{align*}
Similarly,
\begin{align*}
 |\dot z_f|
 &\le\frac{15.24}{4}\frac{15}{8}
 +5+0.7+\frac{10}{4}\frac{288141339}{83886080}\\
 &=\frac{719106075}{33554432}<22.
\end{align*}

For feasible nonnegative-margin paths, gear nonpenetration gives
$z\ge6\cos\phi+6.0579|\sin\phi|\ge6$.
For $\theta\in[0,25^\circ]$, the derivative
$6.0579\cos\theta-6\sin\theta$ is positive, proving the last
inequality.  Thus $V\ge135k$, and the preceding approach bound
gives the entirely rational strict comparison
\[
 (135k)^2-
 \left(\frac{33792275107}{1258291200}\right)^2-64^2
 =\frac{9617823244026244151}{1583296743997440000}>0.
\]
Consequently the horizontal airspeed exceeds $64$ on both phases.
Also $L\ge mg/2$ and $L_u\ge(mg/2)\cos25^\circ$.
These explicit separations exclude the inverse-dynamics
singularities needed in the compactness argument.

\subsection*{Closure without a strict-forward-speed substitution}
The canonical hard forward constraint is $\dot x\ge0$, not
$\dot x>0$.  Suppose $q_j$ is a feasible sequence in the parameter
box and $q_j\to q$.  The positive duration floor gives uniform
convergence of states and derivatives through jerk on each
normalized phase.  The preceding height and airspeed inequalities
first pass to the limit at the kinematic level; hence the moving
basis has no singular limit.  Lift and its upward component then
retain their uniform positive bounds.  All inverse controls,
phasewise rates, finite-reference-cloud distances and conservative
clearance predicates are continuous at $q$.  Every inequality at
every time therefore passes to the limit, as do the finite
terminal and exact ground constraints.  This proves closedness
in the compact box.  For each continuous margin $m$,
\[
 \left|\inf_s m(q,s)-\inf_s m(\widetilde q,s)\right|
 \le\sup_s|m(q,s)-m(\widetilde q,s)|,
\]
so the finite minimum defining the primary objective is continuous.
Nonemptiness supplied by a certified witness yields primary
attainment.  A nonempty closed primary-retention superlevel is
compact, and the normalized-time effort integral is continuous
there, yielding secondary attainment without identifying its
minimizer.
