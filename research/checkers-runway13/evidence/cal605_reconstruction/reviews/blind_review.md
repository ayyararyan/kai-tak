- **P2 — The final anchor has an unbounded upper required-deceleration sensitivity, but the CSV silently represents it as a missing value.** At `reconstruct.py:61`, `rl <= 0` produces `None`. - **P2 — The figure’s shaded “Pixel-reading sensitivity” is not the full declared coordinate sensitivity.** `reconstruct.py:133` shades only y/speed intervals at fixed nominal `rem`, omitting both manual-x and x-calibration uncertainty. A horizontally displaced steep portion of the speed curve can lie outside this band even while all coordinates satisfy the declared tolerances. This matters particularly near the runway end and when visually comparing the trace with stopping boundaries.
