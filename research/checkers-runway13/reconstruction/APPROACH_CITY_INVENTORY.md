# Approach-area historical city inventory

Started 2026-10-13 following Aryan's request to examine the relevant area and establish which buildings existed at a chosen time, for a visible historical approach reconstruction. Initial documentary screening only; no rendered scene or comprehensive building inventory completed.

## Scope and date

Use the late Runway 13 approach area first: inbound Kowloon context, Checkerboard Hill/Kowloon Tsai, the visual-turn neighbourhood around Kowloon City, runway threshold and full airport peninsula. Background hills are needed for the view, not just the ground immediately below a centreline. This is a geographical work area, not a newly defined flight corridor or a claim that every named site lies directly under a flight path.

1993 is the working comparison year, not a newly frozen date. The canonical ledger allows another well-documented operating-airport period. If reproducing CAL605, use 4 November 1993 and resolve within-year demolition/construction. Mountains provide a broadly persistent base, but local excavation, reclamation, slopes and the terrain product's vegetation/infrastructure contamination need checking.

## Newly identified source route

The official Old Survey Sheets collection is distinct from the limited Historical Maps catalogue examined in SOURCE_REPORT.md. Lands Department lists about 72,000 sheets, scales 1:600, 1:1,200 and 1:1,000, published from 1920 onward, scanned and georeferenced to HK1980. These are explicitly general-reference georeferences, not precision survey registration. This improves the acquisition route but does not establish that all required 1993 editions have been retrieved.

- https://www.landsd.gov.hk/en/spatial-data/open-data.html
- https://data.gov.hk/en-data/dataset/hk-landsd-openmap-old-survey-sheets

The official aerial archive extends from 1924 to the present; use exact capture dates and coverage, not catalogue revision dates:
https://www.landsd.gov.hk/en/survey-mapping/mapping/aerial-photo-photogrammetric-products/aerial-photo.html

## Reconstruction procedure

1. Match dated survey sheets and aerial frames to the approach-area work extent.
2. Build historical footprints independently of the current active-building layer; restore demolished structures as well as removing later ones.
3. Record present / absent / construction-or-demolition / unresolved, with source and date interval for each object. Database creation timestamps are not building construction dates.
4. Recover roof elevations separately from presence and footprint. Mark estimated heights explicitly; storeys alone are not measured metres.
5. Reconstruct period airport/coastline and check local earthworks; align horizontal and vertical datums.
6. Render the dated scene and a clearly labelled procedure-consistent camera path. A replay of a specific flight additionally needs that flight's time-position-altitude/attitude evidence; an approach chart does not identify one unique flown trajectory.
