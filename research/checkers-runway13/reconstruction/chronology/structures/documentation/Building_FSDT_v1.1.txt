Foreword


To promote better sharing and accessibility of spatial data among the community of stakeholders in
Hong Kong Special Administrative Region (“HKSAR”), the Government acknowledged its
commitment to establishing Common Spatial Data Infrastructure (“CSDI”). The main objective is to
provide Bureaux/Departments (“B/Ds”) and public and private organisations with an information
infrastructure to share spatial data, support various smart city applications, and support Smart City
development.

In March 2017, the Development Bureau (“DEVB”) commissioned a consultancy study on the overall
strategy for implementing the CSDI. With its implementation, the CSDI would benefit the Government
and society in the long run, with convenient and easy access to high-quality and up-to-date spatial
information. The CSDI will help to:
       Facilitate and support the development of a dynamic and flexible geospatial environment that
        will provide standardised interfaces for accessing and disseminating common spatial
        information;
       Facilitate information sharing across different B/Ds and eventually other stakeholders;
       Encourage the adoption of common standards to ensure quality, compatibility and
        interoperability amongst different systems; and
       Make available a wide range of spatial data and analyses to enable potential applications.

2. Framework Spatial Data
Definitions

2.1. Overview
The Building FSDT covers building structures that enclose spaces including residential, commercial,
industrial, infrastructure, government, institution or community purposes.



2.2. Scope & Definitions
This document for Building covers the following sub-themes:
        Building Outline: Building outlines are features delineating the outline of structures that are
         visible above-ground. Based on existing definitions from DAM Building Common Spatial Unit
         (“CSU”), building outlines are classified as either Tower or Podium. Towers are defined as all
         permanent buildings, open side and temporary structures of a reasonable size as stipulated in
         the latest 1:1000 Basic Mapping Specifications prepared by Lands Department (“LandsD”),
         while podium is defined as containment with facilities for a designated purpose (e.g. shops, car
         park etc.) above ground. Usually, podium together with the tower would form the integral
         building structure.
         To align with Smart City and other strategic innovation initiatives, underground building
         structure1 which include any underground storey of a tower/podium below the ground storey
         with an exit route is also scoped to be covered. The underground extent shall be delineated by
         the maximum extent of all floors, modelled as one or more level, that exist below ground, as
         demarcated in building plans.
        Building Shell: The building shell is based on all the enclosures that represent the exterior of
         the entire building structure, both above the ground and below. It includes all sub-parts of a
         building, such as parts of a building that have different roof heights or multiple towers on a
         single podium.
        Floor: Floors represent the different levels, or stories, within a building. Floors are inclusive of
         units, common areas, stairwells, and other spaces comprising the entire area of a single level,
         or storey.

Content & Structure

3.1. Datasets under Sub-themes
There are five sub-themes within the Building FSDT, namely Building Outline, Building Shell, Floor,
Unit, and Room.

Building Outline sub-theme covers the following dataset(s):
       Building: A polygon showing the building outline of permanent buildings, including tower and
        podium, temporary structures or open-sided structures. Attributes include the building types,
        building names, building height etc.

Building Shell sub-theme covers the following dataset(s):
       3D Visualisation Map (Individualised models): The 3D Visualisation Map (Individualised
        models) are a set of digital data of 3D models featuring geometry models and texture maps to
        represent the geometrical shape, appearance and position of different types of ground objects,
        including building, infrastructure, vegetation, site, waterbody, terrain and generic (others).
       3D Visualisation Map (Tile-based models): The 3D Visualisation Map (Tile-based models) are
        based on the mesh model made from the oblique aerial images.
       3D Visualisation Map (Non-textured models): The 3D Visualisation Map (Non-textured
        Models) is a set of digital data of 3D models featuring geometric models to represent the
        geometrical shape and position of building, infrastructure and terrain.


Floor sub-theme would cover the following dataset(s):
       Floor 3 : A polygon showing the floor inside the building or structure. Floors represent the
        different levels, or stories, within a building. Floors are inclusive of units, common areas,
        stairwells, and other spaces comprising the entire area of a single level, or storey.


Unit sub-theme does not contain any dataset4.


Room sub-theme would cover the following dataset(s):

3.5. Geographical Coverage
The dataset(s) specified in this document covers the entire territories of HKSAR.




3.6. Units of Measurement
The units of measurement used in all datasets under Building FSDT refer to the International System
of Units (“SI”).




3.7. Spatial Resolution/Scale
The spatial resolution(s) or scale(s) of the data specified in this document is/are:
    Building: The Building entity is maintained at a scale of 1: 1000 to show the approximate location
     of the Buildings.
    3D Visualisation Map (Individualised models), 3D Visualisation Map (Tile-based models) and
     3D Visualisation Map (Non-textured models): The dataset is maintained with a horizontal
     accuracy of +/- 0.3m and vertical accuracy of +/- 0.5m to show the approximate location of the
     mesh models.
    3D Visualisation Map (Individualised models) (Grid Index), 3D Visualisation Map (Tile-based
     models) (Grid Index) and 3D Visualisation Map (Non-textured-models): The datasets are
     maintained at a scale of 1: 1000 to show the location of the grids.
    Floor and Room: The datasets are maintained with horizontal and vertical accuracy of +/- 1.0 m
     to show the approximate location of the Floors and Rooms.




3.8. Spatial Representation Type
The spatial representation type(s) of the data specified in this document is/are:
    The Building entity is represented as polygons to represent the geographical location of the entity.
    The 3D Visualisation Map (Individualised models), 3D Visualisation Map (Tile-based models)


                                                                                           Page 23 of 25
     and 3D Visualisation Map (Non-textured-models) dataset are represented as mesh models to
     represent the geographical location of the entity.
    The grid indexes of the 3D Visualisation Map (Individualised models), 3D Visualisation Map
     (Tile-based models) and 3D Visualisation Map (Non-textured-models) are represented as
     polygons with standard dimension to represent the geographical location of the entity.
    The Floor and Room entities are represented as polygons to represent the geographical location of
     the entity.




3.9. Spatial Reference System
The spatial data covered in this document are geo-referenced or projected to one of the Coordinate
Reference Systems as specified in the Simplified Data Specifications for Coordinate Reference System
of the CSDI.
