# Bounded visual observations from official historical aerial frames

Inspected 2026-10-13. These are site-level observations, not a completed building inventory or photogrammetric measurement. Images are oblique/perspective aerial photography, not north-up orthophotos. Catalogue polygons are approximate coverage; do not stretch each image into that polygon and claim accurate building registration.

## Kowloon Walled City transition

- **A23632, 1990-11-13**, 1:8,000, 4,000 ft: the dense roof mass at the known Walled City site is visible near the image right edge, adjacent to the landscaped surrounding open space. This supports **dense built compound present at that capture date**. Roof-level individual structure names and heights are unresolved, and the site is partly near the frame margin.
- **A36142, 1993-11-02**, 1:8,000, 4,000 ft: the site is a largely pale/exposed-ground area with an isolated dark elongated residual structure. The former dense roof mass is no longer present as an intact compound. Supports **demolition/clearance-stage site; residual structures unresolved**, not 'every structure absent'.
- **A36143, 1993-11-02**, 1:8,000, 4,000 ft: the same transition is visible more clearly in the downloaded 3000×3000 DAP-L0 frame, toward the upper-right portion. A large isolated dark-roofed structure and smaller edge features remain; no individual identity assigned. Image border independently prints 2-11-93 and A36143, agreeing with index/product metadata.

The source dates are two days before 1993-11-04, not observations of 4 November itself. No uninterrupted state between dates is established solely by these photos.

The parent sheet investigation found a labelled but internally blank Walled City site in sheet 11-NE-11A, October 1989 edition. Photo evidence demonstrates why **cartographic omission is not proof of absence**. The map edition date is also not necessarily the exact survey/exposure date.

## Checkerboard Hill and broader approach neighbourhood

- **CN4581 (product ID CN04581), 1993-10-08**, 1:8,000, 4,000 ft: full 3000×3000 colour DAP-L0 frame inspected. Painted checkerboard on the hillside is visible beside recreation fields; surrounding low-rise street blocks, tower estates, schools/open grounds and construction sites are visible. The Walled City site near the lower part of the image is predominantly cleared with a residual structure. This provides colour evidence before the November frames, not exact roof elevations or automatic building identities. Printed border reads 8.10.93 and CN4581, agreeing with metadata.

## Airport physical state

- **A36144, 1993-11-02**: runway threshold markings, aprons, taxiways, passenger terminal/boarding structures and many aircraft are clearly visible. Supports historical airport physical layout and airport-building presence at the capture date. The photo alone does not establish traffic operations, structure names, roof heights, or aircraft trajectories.
- **A36143, 1993-11-02**: likewise shows approach end, runway, apron, terminal and aircraft in the full DAP-L0 scan.
- **CN21282, 1998-10-31**: runway/apron/terminal physical infrastructure remains; apron has markedly fewer aircraft than the inspected 1993 frames. Runway crossing markings visible. Do not infer airport's legal operating status solely from image contents.

## Confidence boundaries

- Source date and frame ID are **observed catalogue metadata**, cross-checked against printed frame annotation where readable.
- Landmark-to-frame overlap is **derived from approximate index geometry**. Landmark points are approximate acquisition anchors, not surveyed building coordinates.
- Site-level visible built/cleared appearance is **human/model visual interpretation**, explicitly not automated footprint extraction or validated per-building identity.
- Heights, exact demolition dates, roof geometry, façade detail and every structure's identity remain unmeasured.
- Frame presence/availability is **not** structure classification and does not count as matched individual buildings.
