## Datum discipline: a genuine reconstruction risk now identified

The official notes define HK1980 Grid as Transverse Mercator on HK80, International Hayford 1910 ellipsoid (a=6,378,388 m, inverse flattening 297). Projection origin 22°18′43.68″ N, 114°10′42.80″ E; origin northing 819,069.80 m, easting 836,694.05 m; central-meridian scale 1. The transform PDF explicitly uses ITRF96 at epoch 1998:121; implement its stated matrix/sign convention and validate against its example before applying it. Do not silently identify every modern WGS84 realization with this epoch.

HK Principal Datum **is not mean sea level**. Official notes give MSL approximately 1.23 m above HKPD from 1965–1983 observations, versus approximately 1.30 m from 1997–2015 observations; chart datum is 0.15 m below HKPD. These values demonstrate a mismatch, not an automatically approved conversion for CAD aviation AMSL in 1993. Preserve map HKPD, aviation AMSL, ellipsoidal, radio altitude and runway-relative heights separately until reconciled. Also preserve chart magnetic variation 1°50′W (1990), chart epoch and true/grid/magnetic north distinctions. Original chart horizontal datum remains to be resolved from its source documentation; a graticule is not proof of WGS84.

Tile bounds can be calculated from the viewer's standard Leaflet tile convention, but that only describes its web display georeference; it does not measure the underlying scan's registration error. No heights, footprints or obstacles were inferred from these previews.

Do not redistribute these tiles without settling rights. The official free-photo page explicitly restricts third-party dissemination; its inspected samples are 2015/2018, not 1993. The redistributor's general statements about old Crown materials do not resolve each image's rights.

## Reconstruction now versus next gate

**Available now:** primary historical approach constraints; source-ledger-backed datum conventions; a documented archive acquisition route; narrow 2D preview evidence; existing accident plots for careful digitization. Analytical work can formulate bounds conditional on interval-valued ground/obstacle envelopes without pretending missing geometry is observed.

**Next concrete gate:** retrieve exact 1993 HKMS frame IDs covering Checkerboard Hill, the visual turn and runway, recording capture dates, flight line, flying height, camera/focal length, overlap, availability and rights; retrieve relevant 1:1,000 historical sheet editions with margins/legend/grid and spot heights. The landing date is 4 November 1993; “1993” year labels alone do not establish contemporaneity. Use nearest dated sources and document change windows. Obtain high-resolution stereo imagery and calibration if archive access permits; purchases require authorization. This item-level catalogue work should be attempted before asking Aryan to purchase anything.

Then georeference to stable surveyed controls; reserve independent checkpoints and record residuals; reconcile horizontal/vertical datums; derive ground and roof/obstacle heights separately using stereo and documentary heights, retaining occlusion and demolition/reclamation masks. A footprint plus storey count is a proxy, not observed roof elevation. Modern DEM may support surveyed-stable hills only after change checks; it is not a 1993 coastline/building reconstruction. Neither modern terrain nor heights of present buildings upper-bound all historical obstacles (demolished structures can disappear). Unmeasured roof/crane/obstacle cells must remain unknown or carry justified conservative bounds, never zero height.

**Acceptance before historical feasibility:** sufficient spatial/temporal coverage including missed approach; independently checked datum transform; explicit horizontal/vertical intervals; aircraft swept-volume clearance rather than CG-only clearance; auditable source lineage; unknown cells cannot certify clearance. This is a proposed evidence gate, not a change to the frozen numerical specification.
