# Final editorial assessment — Version 5

## Does it read as a compelling investigation?

**Yes. The revised main manuscript reads as a coherent, engaging engineering investigation, not predominantly as an AI-generated technical report.** That is a judgment about the reading experience, not a claim of unaided human authorship. AI assistance is acknowledged in the manuscript. Neither fluency nor a distinctive voice can establish who wrote a passage.

The improvement is substantive. Version 5 begins with the airport and the directional puzzle, then makes the reader work through the forces, the descent and the runway still needed after touchdown. The historical reconstruction and CAL605 record complicate that calculation at the points where their evidence becomes consequential. The narrative has a physical destination: a stopped aircraft, rather than a completed verification checklist.

## What now works

- **The mathematics supplies the interest.** The force balance explains why mass disappears from the ideal radius relation without disappearing from aircraft performance. Fifteen degrees of bank accumulates 47 degrees of turning over 24.35 seconds. The speed–time rectangle and triangle make stopping distance intelligible before the numerical model appears.
- **Each section changes the problem.** Alignment leads to descent, descent to touchdown state, touchdown to runway consumption, reconstruction to geometric support, and wind to the separation of air and ground motion. “Slower, yet with a harder stop ahead” earns its surprise through the denominator in the stopping requirement.
- **The historical material has identifiable objects.** The survey views locate named forms. The stereo account follows Market features R and S, including their accepted local-plane heights, before explaining the control hull. Its uncertainty now qualifies something the reader has seen.
- **The illustrations perform different explanatory jobs.** Archival frames establish place; a force diagram explains turning; the local-coordinate scene preserves physical scale; the runway graphic allocates distance; the evidence plots distinguish readings, clocks and conditional calculations. They no longer look like one repeated notebook layout.
- **The voice is more direct.** Generic declarations of importance, repeated descriptions of what the paper does, and many redundant negations have been removed. Local scientific conditions remain where they change a result's meaning. Documentary inventories and the full limitations record are accessible in the appendices.

## Residual density and review boundaries

Their objective, exact certificate values and bound distinctions belong to the research argument; removing them would make a smoother essay but a weaker white paper. Act IV still needs a concentrated discussion of datums and incomplete geometry. The concrete roof correspondences make that passage easier to enter, but they do not make the historical-clearance problem simple.

The technical appendices deliberately retain a documentary register: equations, coefficient tables, counts, source conventions, uncertainty categories and rights. They are denser than the main narrative. Their purpose is retrieval and audit, not continuous storytelling. Separate supplementary figure pages retain generous white space; their current typography and labels are readable. These are accepted production choices, not evidence of a return to formulaic main-text prose.

The remaining scope distinctions are substantive: the paths are reduced-model constructions; the two optimized scenarios do not identify a causal wind effect; the historical surface is incomplete; CAL605's causal findings are not displaced by a scalar stopping calculation. Their presence is scientific discipline, not a reason to repeat them throughout the paper.

No independent human aerospace-engineering or aviation-history specialist review has occurred. The independent editorial review is an agent review, and the numerical certificates concern the declared mathematical objects.
