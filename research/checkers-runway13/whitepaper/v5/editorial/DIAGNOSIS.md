# V4 editorial diagnosis

Stage 1 completed 13 October 2026. This is an editorial diagnosis, not new scientific validation. Read: `main.tex`, the foreword, summary, all eight main sections, every figure/table caption, all six appendices, the coefficient tables and references. The actual 35-page PDF was rendered and inspected in nine four-page contact sheets, with individual pages 1 and 7 inspected at larger size. V4 was not changed.

## Verdict

V4 contains a good engineering investigation inside a cautious research-status report. The central calculations are strong enough to carry the paper: 24.35 seconds turns the aircraft through 47 degrees at modest bank; a two-second braking delay consumes 156 metres; a falling speed can accompany a rising stopping requirement. The prose repeatedly interrupts these discoveries to explain the evidential boundaries or the repository. The visual system displays computations competently but rarely constructs a physical scene the reader can reason with.

The strongest existing contribution is the coupling of the airborne path to the full stop, backed by two separate continuous-time certificates. The strongest threat to an editorial rewrite is to make the synthetic landing look historically registered or aircraft-calibrated. Good editing must make that boundary clearer once, not quieter everywhere.

## Ranked editorial problems

1. **The landing puzzle takes too long to begin.** Pages 1–3 deliver an abstract, a long foreword, an executive summary, a results table, a route map and a central-distinction paragraph before the investigation begins. The abstract gives sixteen-digit certificate precision before explaining what margin means. Start with an approximately 200-word abstract, a restrained author-review foreword, then the city and the direction mismatch. Remove the repeated summary from the reading sequence; its scientific content moves to the relevant result sections.
2. **The archive interrupts the first engineering question.** V4 describes 227 archives, 404 assignments, 531 regions, mesh counts and datum gaps before deriving the turning radius. A reader who has just encountered the 47-degree puzzle has not yet been given its answer. Solve the turn, reveal the descent-and-stop problem, then return to the historical city.
3. **Qualifications accumulate rather than govern.** Scope is announced in the abstract, title note, summary, first model description, captions, numbered insights, scenario comparison, five-claim table, synthesis diagram and conclusion. Repetition is not intrinsically safer: it obscures the locally consequential distinctions. `CAVEAT_AUDIT.csv` identifies source locations and specific dispositions. Keep the synthetic-model qualification at its first major result; keep the noncausal comparison at the first paired results; keep measurement/datum definitions with the relevant image. Move proof implementation, invalidated-run history and complete gap inventory to appendices/reports.
4. **The author repeatedly tells the reader why a result matters.** “This is a substantive result”, “make change inspectable”, “the distinction is easily missed” and the seven numbered insights explain the manuscript's intentions. Let the result do the work: show how the runway disappears into delay and braking, how the wind components rotate, and how two photographs recover a roof ridge while leaving a wall unseen.
5. **Methods enter as administration.** “Version 3 adds the repository's settled obstacle handoff” and file/count inventories break the historical voice. Replace them with source-led observations, while retaining all inventory numbers in the technical record. Repository versioning is essential to reproduction, not the plot of the investigation.
6. **The stopping calculation is underused.** It should be the transition from an apparently solved turn to a complete landing. Explain the triangular area under the speed–time curve before presenting the quadratic. The 10%/21% relationship and the cost of one second of delay are memorable without bold “Insight” labels.
7. **The mathematical achievement is introduced defensively.** Explain interval enclosures as a way of preventing a narrow unseen failure between samples. Then state the two exact certificate bounds and their objects. Keep the distinct existence theorem and necessary bounds intact, but move the proof-checker repair history out of the narrative.
8. **The final section dilutes the conclusion.** A public-archive prospectus follows the result-and-limitations discussion. Rights, file layers and licence status belong in a reproducibility appendix/report. End with the physical synthesis and the specific empirical challenge of putting this mathematics back into the dated city.
9. **The foreword overexplains its own authenticity.** Its approximately one-page length lets disclaimers and the paper's outline displace the supplied personal motivation. Preserve childhood aviation, *Engineering Connections*, *Enter the Dragon*, PolyU and personal Hong Kong connection without inventing an incident or emotion. About 220–270 words, author approval label retained.

## Page-level observations

The Times-based RFS proportions, running heads, mathematical type and restrained tables are satisfactory and should be retained. Small figure type is the dominant legibility risk, especially pp. 6, 8, 10–11 and 18–19. The 3D panel on p. 14 makes the runway a thin line in an empty cube despite having enough page area for a physical scene. Page 7 is almost entirely counts and qualifications. Figure captions commonly consume many lines without giving a clear opening sentence about the visual. The ending straddles pp. 27–28 just before technical derivations. The coefficient tables are useful reproducibility material and should remain technical, not be made into decorative narrative figures.

## Non-negotiable editorial safeguards

- No new model, fitted quantity, historical coordinate or source right is created by rewriting.
- Keep 47-degree *direction difference*, magnetic references and final visual acquisition correct.
- Keep the synthetic entry/corridor and uncalibrated envelope explicit at the first constructed landing.
- Keep wind cases separately optimized, certificates distinct and upper bounds necessary within finite families.
- Retain the brief climb in both paths; no cosmetic monotonic descent.
- Keep CAL605's controllable-touchdown finding and official rollout causes authoritative.
- Keep relative stereo heights, incomplete control support, datum gap and missing obstacle volumes visible where interpreted.
- Keep exact technical details in the appendices/claim ledger even when removed from the abstract or storyline.
