Neither option overrides upstream Hong Kong data conditions or grants rights to CAD/Boeing scans.

The white paper's scholarly-publication license is a separate decision. A citation recommendation is not a license. Research credit does not establish copyright ownership. Mixed source overlays, image crops, supplied photograph and publisher figures require item-specific review. No license is applied automatically to a whole research directory.
