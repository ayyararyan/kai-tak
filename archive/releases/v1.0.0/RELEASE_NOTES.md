# Data Release v1.0.0 — unpublished candidate

**The Kai Tak Historical Research Archive**

Historical Geography, Aviation Evidence, Flight Dynamics and Mathematical Reconstructions of Hong Kong's Kai Tak Airport.

This is the first archive-version series, independent of paper/model versions and the three retained 2026-10-13 GitHub tags. No release, DOI or new license grant has been published.

The candidate describes 3,268 canonical artifacts in 44 datasets and nine stable collections. Five complete, independently restorable data bundles contain 418 verified-permitted files (1,305,783,427 uncompressed bytes): acquired map ZIPs/full DAP-L0 photographs, source-linked modern structure data, terrain/geospatial material, NOAA observations and registered raster derivatives. See [master manifest](manifest.json), [checksums](SHA256SUMS) and the exact collection manifests. Collections 04, 07, 08 and 09 are catalogue-only in this candidate.

The 2,850 other catalogue artifacts are explicit exceptions, not promised downloads. Many exist in the inherited Git source or old releases, but historic publication is not evidence of a new redistribution license. Original-work grant decisions, CAD/AIS/Boeing permission, HKMS historical-index/preview rights and missing exact lineage remain unresolved. New metadata/code/support files are prepared for review, not newly open-licensed.

Modern building data are not complete 1993 geometry. Photo-index rows are not full frames. Registered raster outputs have pinned source/output hashes, not independently validated survey accuracy. Synthetic trajectories establish neither complete historical obstacle clearance nor global optimality or operational safety. Seven explicitly invalidated analytical JSON results are identified in metadata without changing original bytes. Three inherited v1 evidence-manifest discrepancies and historical source-version gaps are retained in the audit.

For verified execution evidence and blockers use the [publication-readiness report](../../PUBLICATION_READINESS.md). Corrections before publication remain candidate revisions; once accepted/published, any changed data require a new semantic version with retained prior bytes and correction notes.
